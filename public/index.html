How "For you" works

Like (8) / Dislike (2) teach it your taste; Super like (9) and Super dislike (3) count much more. The more you vote, the better the picks. A few votes in a row also shift what you see right now, a little.

Balanced: mostly things close to your likes, with a surprise every 4th post.
Comfort: stays close to what you already like, few surprises.
Explore: lots of new things, every 2nd post is a discovery.

Images / Videos: only show that media type.
🧭 Deep Dive: on any post, shows posts similar to just that one. Your votes barely change your long-term taste while diving. Press End to leave.

Each post says why it was picked. Batches are small (set in Settings) so each vote improves the next batch. Settings → For you shows what it has learned and whether it is working.
